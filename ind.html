🧠 How Students Learn

The landing page is the front door to the session-by-session learning journey.

🤖 ELI10 Learning

Begin with familiar stories and simple mental models, then move toward technical cybersecurity concepts.

🔍 Detective Thinking

Observe, question, classify, compare, verify and defend reasoning instead of simply memorizing definitions.

📊 Real-Time Learning

Interactive activities, classroom decisions, quizzes and evidence-based observations can be incorporated into each mission.
