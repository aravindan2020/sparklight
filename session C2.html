🏠 1. Imagine Your Computer Is a House

🤖 ELI10 STORY

Imagine you have a beautiful house. Inside your house you have:

  • 💰 Money
  • 📚 Books
  • 📱 Phone
  • 💻 Laptop
  • 📸 Family photos

You lock the doors because you don't want strangers to enter.

Now imagine your computer is the same house.

The computer contains your: photos, passwords, documents, applications and data.

Cybersecurity protects this digital house.

🧠 Simple Rule:

Cybersecurity = Protecting digital things from digital danger.

⚔️ 2. Threat vs Attack vs Vulnerability vs Risk

Students often confuse these four words. Let's make them super simple.

Word ELI10 Meaning House Example Computer Example
Threat Something that could cause harm Thief outside Malware
Vulnerability A weakness Broken window Unpatched software
Attack The actual harmful action Thief entering Exploiting a weakness
Risk Chance of something bad happening Chance thief enters Chance vulnerability is exploited
Threat Actor Who causes the trouble Thief Cyber attacker

🧠 Remember with ONE sentence:

Threat = danger, Vulnerability = weakness, Attack = harmful action, Risk = chance of harm, Threat Actor = person/group behind it.

👾 5. Ten Common Cyber Threat Examples

🎣

1. Phishing

Fake messages try to trick people into revealing information or clicking unsafe links.

🦠

2. Malware

Malicious software designed to perform harmful actions.

🔒

3. Ransomware

Malicious software that can make data unavailable and demand payment.

🌊

4. DDoS

A service is overwhelmed with huge amounts of traffic.

👂

5. Sniffing

Network traffic may be observed to understand communication.

🎭

6. Spoofing

Someone or something pretends to be another trusted source.

🔑

7. Credential Theft

Attackers attempt to obtain usernames, passwords or tokens.

🕳️

8. Vulnerability Exploitation

An attacker attempts to take advantage of a software weakness.

🧑‍💻

9. Insider Threat

A person with legitimate access misuses that access.

🤖

10. Automated Attack

Software can automatically perform large numbers of security-related actions.

📱

11. Mobile Threat

Phones and mobile applications can also contain security weaknesses.

☁️

12. Cloud Threat

Poor configuration or stolen credentials may expose cloud resources.

⚖️ 8. Ethical Hacker vs Malicious Hacker

🟢 Ethical Hacker

  • Has permission
  • Defines the testing scope
  • Documents findings
  • Reports vulnerabilities
  • Helps improve security

🔴 Malicious Attacker

  • Does not have permission
  • Attempts unauthorized access
  • May steal information
  • May damage systems
  • May hide activity
🧠 ELI10:

Imagine two children testing a school door.

👦 Child A has permission from the teacher to check whether the door lock works.

👤 Child B secretly breaks the lock and enters.

Both interact with the door, but their permission and purpose are completely different.

🕳️ 10. What Is a Vulnerability?

🏠 House Example

Imagine your house has a window. The window does not close properly.

That window is a weakness.

A thief might notice it.

In cybersecurity, a software weakness is called a vulnerability.

Real World Cyber World
Broken door Software vulnerability
Open window Incorrect configuration
Lost key Stolen password
No security guard No monitoring
Old lock Outdated software

🎲 11. What Is Cybersecurity Risk?

ELI10:

You have a house with an open window.

Is the house definitely going to be robbed?

No.

But there is a possibility. That possibility is part of the risk.

Situation Weakness? Threat? Risk?
Old software installed Yes Possible attacker Possible security problem
Strong updated software Lower Threats still exist Usually lower
Weak password Yes Password attacker Higher
MFA enabled Lower account exposure Threat still exists Generally reduced

🎭 12. Social Engineering — Hacking the Human

Sometimes attackers do not attack the computer first.

They attack the person's trust.

Imagine someone says:

"Hi! I am from your school IT department. Please tell me your password so I can fix your account."

The computer may be perfectly secure. But if the person gives away the password, the attacker may gain access.

Technique ELI10 Explanation Warning Sign
Phishing Fake digital message Suspicious link
Pretexting Fake story to gain trust Unusual request
Impersonation Pretending to be someone trusted Unexpected identity claim
Urgency Trick Trying to make you panic "Do it immediately!"

🕵️ 13. Become a Cyber Detective

Look at each situation and identify the likely security problem.

🔎 Example 1

You receive an email saying: "Your account will be deleted in 10 minutes!" It contains a strange link.

What should you suspect?

🎯 Possible phishing attempt.
🔎 Example 2

A student uses the password: 123456

🎯 Weak password / authentication risk.
🔎 Example 3

A computer has not received security updates for a very long time.

🎯 Possible vulnerability due to outdated software.
🔎 Example 4

An employee receives a phone call from someone pretending to be technical support and asking for credentials.

🎯 Social engineering / impersonation.
🔎 Example 5

A website suddenly receives a massive amount of traffic and becomes unavailable.

🎯 Possible denial-of-service activity.

⚔️ 15. Threat vs Vulnerability — Don't Confuse Them!

👾 THREAT

"Something that could hurt me."

  • Attacker
  • Malware
  • Phishing
  • Natural disaster
  • Insider misuse

🕳️ VULNERABILITY

"A weakness that could be used."

  • Weak password
  • Old software
  • Bad configuration
  • Missing security control
  • Programming weakness

🧠 Easy Memory Trick

👾 Threat = Bad guy / danger
🕳️ Vulnerability = Weak door
⚔️ Attack = Breaking through the door
🎲 Risk = Chance of trouble

🎯 17. Classroom Activity — Cyber Threat Detective

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Group Challenge

Divide students into small teams. Give every team 5 scenarios.

For every scenario students must identify:

  1. What is the asset?
  2. What is the threat?
  3. What is the vulnerability?
  4. What could be the attack?
  5. What protection could reduce the risk?
Question Student Answer
🏠 What are we protecting? Asset
👾 What could cause harm? Threat
🕳️ What is weak? Vulnerability
⚔️ What harmful action could happen? Attack
🛡️ How can we reduce danger? Security Control
